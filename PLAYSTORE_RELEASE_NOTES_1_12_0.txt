VELOCITY RIDES 1.12.0  ·  Android versionCode 37  ·  iOS build 10
=================================================================

1.11.0 is live on the App Store (build 9). Apple takes no new build against a
released version, so this release is 1.12.0 on both platforms; app.json holds
one version string for both. EAS assigns the build numbers.

The store notes below introduce Velocity Rides as if to someone opening it for
the first time. From this release on, people are expected to download and
start using it. Every line in them describes something this build really does,
so the notes cannot become a review problem:
  - no competitor names;
  - no "best", "#1", "cheapest" or other claims we cannot back up;
  - no "coming soon";
  - the App Store text never mentions Android, and the Play text never
    mentions the App Store.

APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (already set on version 1.12.0 through the API)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Welcome to Velocity Rides: getting around Pakistan on a fare you agree on.

NAME YOUR FARE
Offer a price for your trip. Nearby drivers accept it or send their own offer, and you choose who to ride with.

A RIDE FOR EVERY TRIP
Moto, Mini, A/C and Premium rides across town, City to City seats for longer journeys, and couriers when only a parcel needs to travel.

SHARE THE RIDE, SPLIT THE FARE
Share a car with people heading your way and each pay for one seat. You see who is already in the car before you join, and shared rides follow gender rules: for example, women are only placed with men if they have chosen to allow it.

BOOK BY VOICE
Say where you want to go, in Urdu or English, and the booking fills itself in.

PAY THE WAY YOU ALREADY PAY
Cash, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer or your Velocity wallet.

SAFETY ON EVERY RIDE
Drivers verify their identity and their car before they can drive. Follow your driver live on the map, share your trip with someone you trust, and raise an SOS if anything feels wrong.

TRAVEL PARTNER
Find people going the same way, join city groups and plan trips together.

FOR DRIVERS
Drive solo or take shared rides, choose how far from your route you will go for a pickup, and add riders who are on your way, even during a trip.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.11.0 (for the team; not store copy)
--------------------------------------------------------
In the app, new in this build:
  - Drivers can open "Pick up riders on your way" during a trip. The button
    used to disappear the moment the ride started, although the backend
    always allowed it.
  - Drivers choose their pickup radius on that screen: 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2 km,
    capped by the admin maximum. The list and "Pick them up" use the same
    radius.
  - Pool invite links say why you cannot join (for example the car's gender
    rules) instead of failing at the join.
  - Travel Partner shared-ride links show "waiting for the driver" when the
    driver has to approve.
  - The booking screen shows the new third-seat share: three riders each pay
    45% of the solo fare (was 40%, which earned the driver nothing for the
    third rider).
  - Partner earnings show paisa, so small commissions are no longer Rs 0.

Already live on the backend since 27 Sep 2026; these reach every build:
  - "Pick them up" works. Every call used to fail on a Firestore write error.
  - The same gender rules on every way into a car:
      - instant join;
      - driver approval;
      - Travel Partner link;
      - pickup on the way;
      - the suggested-rides feed.
  - Drivers see their real extra earnings; per-city fares; each franchise's
    own rate; Pro plan expiry enforced.
  - Velocity's own map: suggestions carry a unique id, so every build lists
    and resolves them correctly.
