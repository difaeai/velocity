(Build numbers are EAS's remote auto-increment; the build list is the truth.)

WHY 1.25.0. iOS 1.24.0 (build 30) is READY_FOR_SALE, read from the App Store
Connect API on 6 Nov before choosing, so its train is closed. It also has to be
a store build, not an OTA update: the launcher icon, splash and Android adaptive
icon changed, and those live in the native binary.

WHAT IT CARRIES (PR #166).
  - New logo everywhere: the glowing V on wheels, from one master
    (apps/mobile/assets/brand-render.png); sign-in's lightning bolt is gone.
  - Liquid glass: Apple's own glass on iOS 26, a drawn sheen elsewhere.
  - Drivers: Corporate & Students | All. Corporate shows Right now (green) and
    Coming up next (red). Sign-up asks for a WhatsApp number; drivers without
    one are asked on home.
  - Shared-ride WhatsApp alerts name the riders and the whole car's fare.
  - Ride sharing: My Routes, Favourites (heart or double tap), what leaves near
    you first, and each route on a real map.
No native module was added (expo-glass-effect was already in the build).

APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on a NEW 1.25.0 version record)
-----------------------------------------------------------
A fresh new look and easier ride sharing.

Velocity Rides has a new logo and a glassy new design across the app.

Ride sharing is quicker to use: save your daily route under My Routes, keep the routes you use most in Favourites with a heart or a double tap, and see every route drawn on a real map. Rides leaving near you now come first.

For drivers: Corporate & Students now shows rides by time, what is leaving right now and what is coming up next. Add your WhatsApp number to hear about rides even when the app is closed, and shared ride alerts now tell you how many riders are going and the full fare.
