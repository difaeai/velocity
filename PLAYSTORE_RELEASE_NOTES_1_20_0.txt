APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on a NEW 1.20.0 version record)
-----------------------------------------------------------
CALL YOUR DRIVER FOR FREE

Every Call button on a ride now opens a voice call over the internet instead of
dialling your SIM. It costs no airtime, your phone number stays private, and it
only works between the people on the same live ride.

A HELPLINE THAT ANSWERS

Call the helpline from the app and an assistant answers in Urdu or English,
already knowing whether you are a rider or a driver. If it cannot sort it out,
it hands you to our team with the conversation attached.

FLEETS: RIDE WITH YOUR OWN PEOPLE

Pick a username, add the people you travel with, and ask them all at once —
"I'm going to Gulberg now, coming?". Everyone who says yes is seated before a
driver is asked, so the ride leaves full. Find it under Corporate / Students.

A DASH CAM ON EVERY RIDE

The driver's phone records the cabin for the whole ride. You can watch your own
part of it for 24 hours after the ride ends; then it is deleted.

ALSO

Sign-up is one screen: username, photo, name, gender and date of birth
together. Drivers see their mobile-holder credits at the top of the home screen.
And the app now recovers from errors with a retry screen instead of closing.
