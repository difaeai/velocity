🔀 SHARED RIDES THAT ACTUALLY GET PICKED UP

Sharing a ride only saves you money if a driver takes it. Until now a shared
ride sat in the same feed as everything else, described in a single line, at a
fare the driver had to haggle over — so most of them were skipped. This release
rebuilds the driver's side of sharing, lets a pool form before it has a driver,
and drops the price of the cheapest ride on Velocity.

✨ WHAT'S NEW

🏍️ MOTO RIDES, AND A LOWER PRICE
- Bike rides are now called Moto
- A Moto starts at Rs 100 instead of Rs 150
- Fares across every vehicle have been recalibrated against what Yango and
  inDrive charge for the same journey, so what you see quoted is in line with
  the rest of the market

🔀 DRIVERS GET A SHARING RIDES MODE
- Right below Offline/Online, drivers now choose between Sharing Rides and
  Solo Rides — and Sharing Rides is where the app starts
- Sharing Rides lays every nearby pool out as a box a driver can actually
  judge: each rider by first name with their fare, what the pool pays now, what
  it pays if it fills, the pickup and destination areas, and where each joiner
  gets out
- The driver picks the radius they want to see — 2 km, 5 km or 10 km
- Pool fares are fixed. The driver accepts or rejects; there is no counter-offer
  and no haggling over your seat

👥 JOIN A POOL BEFORE IT HAS A DRIVER
- You no longer have to wait for a driver to be assigned before you can join a
  shared ride — you can join at the proposed fare while the pool is still
  looking
- The ride shows you which stage it is at: finding a driver, or driver assigned
- A pool that fills up without a driver stays open, so a driver can still take it
- When you join you get exactly two choices for where you get out: the same area
  as the pool's destination, or your own drop-off within 2 km of it

⏱️ TEN MINUTES, THEN YOU BOTH DECIDE
- If ten minutes pass after a driver accepts and nobody else has joined, you and
  the driver are asked the same question at the same time: go anyway, or cancel
- Going ahead needs both of you to agree. Either one of you can cancel
- Both screens count the ten minutes down live, so nobody is left guessing

💬 WHATSAPP RIDE ALERTS FOR DRIVERS
- A driver with the app closed gets no notification, so ride requests go unseen
  by drivers two streets away. Drivers can now opt in to a WhatsApp alert instead
- Strictly opt-in, from the new "WhatsApp ride alerts" screen in the driver
  drawer. Nothing is switched on for anybody automatically
- Drivers set the number the alerts go to, which is often not the number they
  drive on, and see the masked number we recorded so a mistyped digit is caught
  straight away
- A few a day at most, never between 10pm and 7am, only for real rides, and
  STOP works at any time — in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu

📊 WHAT ELSE IMPROVED
- The code resend timer now runs a full 59 seconds, so the app stops offering a
  new code before the network has had time to deliver the first one
- The driver menu scrolls, and its header takes up less room, so every entry is
  reachable on a small phone
- Tidied up the driver screens: the voice-command button has been removed from
  the driver side, where it got in the way. Voice booking is unchanged for
  passengers

🚀 NEXT UP

- More payment options
