VELOCITY RIDES 1.14.0  ·  Android versionCode 39  ·  iOS build 12
=================================================================

1.13.0 was built (vc38, iOS build 11) but never uploaded to either store, so
nothing in it has reached a user. This release carries 1.13.0's two changes AND
everything merged since, under one version number: app.json holds one version
string for both platforms and EAS assigns the build numbers.

The release people will notice is safety. Three of the four changes are about
what happens when something goes wrong -- the emergency numbers, a family
member watching the ride, and a complaint that gets answered. The fourth is for
drivers: a daily target worth PKR 2,000.

APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.14.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

SAFETY CENTRE
One screen for every emergency, from the menu or from a live ride. Police 15
and Rescue 1122 first, and reporting an online scam goes to both Velocity and
the NCCIA cybercrime helpline (1799). Nothing dials on its own -- you confirm
every call.

SHARE YOUR RIDE WITH FAMILY
Send a link and someone you trust can follow the trip in any browser: no app,
no account. They see the car on the map, how fresh that position is, the plate
and the driver's number. The link dies when the ride does, and you can revoke
it at any time.

HELP THAT ANSWERS
Velocity Rapid Response replaces the old help chat. Your question gets an
answer straight away, and a real person takes over the moment you ask for one
-- or the moment it is about safety or your money. Nothing is left unanswered.

FOR DRIVERS: A DAILY TARGET
Complete 15 rides in a day and you earn a PKR 2,000 bonus, and that day's
rides cost you no commission at all. The bonus pays your Velocity charges for
you, day after day, until it runs out.

Your sign-in code also arrives faster, and entering your date of birth is now
three quick taps: day, month and year.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD (for the team; not store copy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
New in this build (merged 1-2 Oct 2026):
  - Daily ride target. 15 qualifying rides in one PAKISTAN day (UTC+05:00)
    earns PKR 2,000 and waives that day's commission. The money is a BONUS,
    never withdrawable: it pays Velocity's own charges until it runs out. Every
    number -- target, bonus, rate, threshold, waiver, three anti-farming
    minimums -- is admin-settable on the Commission page, and the driver app
    STREAMS that config, so a change is live in open apps within a second with
    no release. Stored fields keep the older `commissionCredit` names on
    purpose; only the labels changed.
  - Live trip links. /watch/{token} is a real web page for someone with no app
    and no account. getTripWatch is unauthenticated by design -- the token IS
    the credential -- and returns a hand-built redacted view, so adding a field
    to a trip can never widen what a shared link shows. Expires with the ride
    (3h grace), revocable per link, rate-limited per token.
  - Safety Centre on both sides, plus the police. Dialling 15 on a live ride
    raises a silent `police_called` event after the call; critical events push
    every admin with the position, the plate and both numbers.
  - Velocity Rapid Response. supportTickets replaces supportChats: one ticket
    per complaint, AI first, a person the moment you ask. Escalation triggers
    on the raw words (English, Urdu script and Roman Urdu) BEFORE any API call,
    on category (safety and scams never belong to a machine), on the model's
    own judgement and on the clock. A customer message is never left without a
    reply -- no key, a refusal, a timeout and bad JSON all still answer and
    queue a human. A 15-minute sweep catches anything missed.
  - Driver wording: what a driver EARNS is a bonus, never a commission, on
    every surface a driver can read -- app, site, admin, pushes and the support
    agent's brief. Urdu follows (آپ کا بونس).
  - Composite indexes for the three new support queries, without which the
    customer's ticket list and the stale-queue sweep fail at runtime.

Carried over from 1.13.0, which never shipped:
  - Sign-in wakes the server before it is needed. Production logs showed nearly
    every sign-in meeting a sleeping server: 4.8 s typical, up to 11 s, against
    1.6 s when awake.
  - Date of birth is Day / Month / Year tiles instead of the native calendar.

Verified before the build: mobile typecheck clean, 138/138 mobile tests pass.
Backend at this commit: 752 tests / 55 files, Firestore rules 29/29.
