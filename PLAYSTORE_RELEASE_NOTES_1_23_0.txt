APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on a NEW 1.23.0 version record)
-----------------------------------------------------------
RIDE SHARING, REBUILT

Tap + to share your daily ride: the place name, your drop-off and pickup, the
start time and the fare. Every ride shows as a small box with the car, the
seats left and the fare, and a green light while seats are free.

Search where you are going: rides on your route come first, then the ones
ending nearest to you. Tap All to see every ride in your city. Each box shows
whether women or men are riding, because a shared car is always one gender.

Join more than one ride to be sure of a seat. When a driver starts one,
confirm it in one tap and your other seats are let go. Leave any ride from its
own box.

DRIVERS

See the shared rides near you and how many people are going. Tap Start when
you set off: riders confirm, and your list shows pickups and drop-offs nearest
first, with each rider's fare.
