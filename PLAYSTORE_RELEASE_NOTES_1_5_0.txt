📣 SEE THE ADVERTISING BEFORE YOU BUY IT

Velocity lets a local business pay to put their offer on the phones of people
who pass their door. Until now the only way to find out what that notification
looks like was to buy it. This release lets you send it to yourself.

✨ WHAT'S NEW

📣 "SEE IT ON YOUR PHONE" — FIND YOUR CUSTOMERS
- A new button on the Find your Customers screen sends you a sample offer, as a
  real notification, with a real picture
- It is the same notification your own offer would go out as: your business
  name, your offer, your picture, and how far away the customer is
- Send it now to see what it looks like, or send it in 10 seconds so you can
  close Velocity first and watch it arrive on a locked phone — which is how your
  customers will actually get it
- Pull your notification shade down and it is there, waiting, with the app shut
- Tap it and the full offer opens, with the address, the distance and the times
- Press it as many times as you like. It goes to your phone and nobody else's,
  it costs nothing, and it does not use up anybody's daily notification limit

🧾 A NOTE ON THE SAMPLE
- The sample offer is clearly marked DEMO. It is an illustration of the format,
  not a real promotion from the restaurant it names

📊 WHAT ELSE IMPROVED
- Notification permission is now requested at the moment you ask for the demo,
  and if notifications are switched off the app says so plainly instead of
  leaving you waiting for something that cannot arrive

🚀 NEXT UP
- Driver voice booking
- App-wide security attestation (App Check)
- More payment options

Available now in your Velocity app! 🎉

Questions? Tap Support from Settings for help.
