VELOCITY RIDES 1.10.0  ·  Android versionCode 35  ·  iOS build 8
=================================================================

This is the first Play release since 1.8.0 (vc30). vc31 and vc34 were built but
never rolled out, so everything they carried ships here. On iOS it is the first
update since 1.9.0 (build 7) went live on 2026-09-22.


PLAY CONSOLE "WHAT'S NEW" (copy the block below, max 500 chars)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Every chat now has a menu to leave the conversation, block the person, or
report them to our safety team — in Travel Partner chats and group chats alike.

Also: one Messages inbox for partners, drivers and brands; see what inDrive and

WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.8.0
------------------------

Chat management — leave, block, report
  Every Travel Partner conversation now has a menu in the top corner: view the
  person's profile, report them, block them, or leave the chat.

    - Leaving a one-to-one chat closes it for both people and takes it out of
      your Messages. A two-person conversation ends when one person walks out;
      pretending otherwise would just leave the other side talking to nobody.
    - Leaving a group removes you from it, and you stop seeing its messages.
      The group carries on without you and is told that you left.
    - Blocking someone stops them messaging you, finding you or seeing your
      posts, anywhere in Travel Partner. In a group it hides them from you —
      it cannot throw another member out of a room you both joined.
    - Reporting asks what happened (harassment, threats, a scam, spam, a fake
      profile, and so on), and can block them in the same step. Our safety team
      sees the conversation as it was when you reported it, so deleting the
      chat afterwards cannot erase what was said.

  Ride chat with your driver deliberately has none of this. Once a ride is
  booked the driver is on their way to you and you both need to stay reachable
  until the trip ends. Safety there is the SOS button and the disputes desk.

One Messages inbox
  Travel Partner chats, your driver, and replies from businesses now live in
  one Messages screen with three sections, each carrying its own unread count.
  It replaces "My questions". A finished ride's conversation is reachable there
  without going back to the trip.

See what the other apps charge
  The booking screen shows what inDrive and Yango would cost for the same
  route, so you can tell whether your offer is reasonable before you send it.

Pay how you actually pay
  Cash, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer or wallet — pick one or several when
  you book. Wallet top-ups are still switched off.

Drivers photograph the car they are driving
  A driver registers each of their vehicles and confirms with a photo which one
  they are in. The confirmation expires, so the car on your screen is the car
  that arrives. Drivers can add and switch between their own cars.

Find your Customers
  The business advertising screen was rebuilt: a clearer radius and budget, a
  "Seen by" count of the people who actually opened the ad, and a Queries inbox
  so a business can answer questions about its own offer. Abusive senders can
  be reported and blocked from there too.

  The drawer entry is now "Find my Customers" and opens the ad screen directly.

Velocity Rides
  The app, the website and every message we send now say "Velocity Rides"
  rather than "Velocity". Nothing about your account changes.

Now on iPhone
  Velocity Rides is on the App Store, and the app knows which store to send you
  to when there is an update. Same account, same rides, same cash payments.

Smaller things
  - The turning V splash is back, and no longer clips on some phones.
  - Sign-in codes over WhatsApp send correctly.
  - Release builds are obfuscated, which Play had been flagging.
  - Dead links, a stuck admin queue and two slow "Find my Customers" loads are
    fixed.
