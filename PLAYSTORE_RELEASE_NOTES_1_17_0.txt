APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.17.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

SHARED RIDES COME TO ONE DRIVER AT A TIME

A shared ride used to appear in every nearby driver's list at once, and the
first driver to tap it got it. That rewarded whoever had the fastest phone and
the best signal, and it meant the four hours you had already spent online
counted for nothing.

Now a ride is offered to one driver, exclusively, for 25 seconds, and the
driver asked first is the one who has been online longest in that area. There
is a countdown on the card so you can see the ride is actually yours.

AND YOU CAN SAY NO

Passing on a ride is a real answer now. Tap Pass and the ride goes immediately
to the next driver in the queue — it does not sit in your list, and it does not
come back. Ignoring an offer does the same thing when the 25 seconds run out,
just more slowly.

If nobody takes it, the ride opens up to every driver nearby rather than
waiting. No rider is ever left holding a ride the queue could not place.

SOLO MODE IS RESPECTED

Choosing Solo on your home screen now means you are not offered shared rides
at all, instead of being handed pools you did not want.

FOR RIDERS: THE FARE EXPLAINS ITSELF

Fuel prices in Pakistan change by government notification, and Velocity already
moved its per-kilometre rates with them. The price on your booking screen now
moves with them too, and tells you the pump price it was worked out from. When
an area is busy you also see the busy factor and, next to it, the most it can
ever add — because a price that rises without a stated ceiling is the thing
nobody trusts about ride-hailing.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD (for the team; not store copy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
One merge off main, 12 Oct 2026: d027a89 (87caa6a inside it).

  - poolRideRequests/dispatchLadder.ts (pure) + dispatch.ts (Firestore) +
    sweepDispatch.ts. A pool is offered to one driver for 25s, ordered by the
    SAME zone queue that gates placeBid; refusal or lapse records the driver in
    dispatch.declined and advances. Exits to broadcast after 8 offers, 240s, or
    when no candidate is left — an ordering policy must never become a
    precondition for the marketplace working.
  - driverRespondToRequest takes action:'reject'. Before this the enum was
    'accept' | 'counter' (counter is dead), so a driver had no way to say no.
    The Reject button existed on BOTH driver screens and wrote only to
    AsyncStorage — a refusal was known to one handset and nothing else.
  - Candidates are filtered by every gate the accept path enforces (gender, car
    photo, commission owing, pool membership) so a rung is never spent on a
    driver who would be refused at the last moment. Plus the new
    drivers/{uid}.rideMode: 'solo' drivers are skipped, and ABSENT must keep
    meaning available — no build already on a phone writes it.
  - A missing `dispatch` map means BROADCAST, not "empty ladder". Every pool
    created before this shipped has no such field, and reading it as empty
    would have hidden the whole open-pool backlog from every driver on deploy.
    Same care in firestore.rules: the offered-driver read uses
    resource.data.get('dispatch', {}).get('offeredDriverId', '') because a
    missing-field access in rules is an ERROR, not a false, and in an || chain
    it denies the whole read — it would have broken admin reads of that backlog.
  - getFareContext (fare/fareFunctions.ts) returns the FUEL-ADJUSTED rate card
    plus the zone's peak factor in one call. booking.tsx read
    fareConfig/{CITY_ID} straight from Firestore and priced with the app's own
    engine; that document is deliberately never fuel-adjusted, so the pricing
    engine moved what the server CHARGED and left the rider's screen pricing
    off the un-adjusted table, with PriceNotice.tsx built and imported nowhere.
    One call, not one per vehicle class — the screen prices every class locally
    and switching class must stay instant.
  - That also closed a live overcharge seam: offeredFareBounds is
    fuel-adjusted, so once the notified pump price rose ABOVE the configured
    baseline, the rider's auto-filled offer could fall under the server's
    minAcceptableBid and be clamped up — charged more than the screen showed.
    Today's price sits below baseline, which is the only reason it had not
    surfaced yet.
  - booking.tsx hardcoded CITY_ID = 'islamabad_rawalpindi' while every server
    path resolves the city from the pickup coordinates. A Karachi rider was
    shown Islamabad's rates AND held against Islamabad's competitor card. The
    city now comes from the server with CITY_ID only as the no-location
    fallback.
  - One composite index added (poolRideRequests status + dispatch
    .offerExpiresAt) for the sweeper. The deploy pipeline's index step is
    continue-on-error, so this one was verified by running the sweeper's exact
    query against production rather than trusting a green run.

Verified before the build: backend 1069 tests / 71 files on the Firestore
emulator (74 new), all 34 security-rules tests, both typechecks clean, backend
eslint clean. CI and Deploy Cloud Functions both green on d027a89, and
getFareContext + sweepPoolDispatch are confirmed live in asia-south1 — so the
ladder and the adjusted card are ALREADY serving on the server. This build is
the app half: the Pass button that tells the server, the countdown, rideMode,
and PriceNotice.

NOTE ON THE PRICING ENGINE'S FIRST RUN: as of this build fuelPrices/current did
not exist in production — refreshFuelPrices is scheduled 06:15 Asia/Karachi and
had not had a slot since it deployed. Until it runs (or an admin taps Refresh
on Admin → Pricing engine) the fuel factor is 1.0 and fares stand exactly at
the authored rate card. That is the designed fallback, not a fault.
