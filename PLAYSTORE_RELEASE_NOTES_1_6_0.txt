🔀 SHARING A RIDE, WHERE YOU CAN ACTUALLY SEE IT

Sharing a ride has always been the cheapest way to travel on Velocity. It was
also the hardest thing in the app to find, and once you were in a shared car
neither you nor your driver was told much about it. This release fixes all of
that.

✨ WHAT'S NEW

🔀 SHARED RIDES NOW COME FIRST
- Rides already heading your way appear at the TOP of the booking screen, before
  you price anything
- They used to sit below the fare — you had to scroll past every decision you
  were making to find the option that cost less
- Every ride in the list shows the driver, the car, who is already aboard and
  what your seat would cost

✅ ONLY REAL RIDES, WITH A REAL DRIVER
- A shared ride is offered to you only after its driver is confirmed and the
  fare is agreed
- No more joining a ride that was still being haggled over, at a price that
  could still move, that could disappear before it started
- Open an invite too early and Velocity tells you it is still finding a driver,
  and lets you check back — instead of pretending the ride has gone

👥 YOU KNOW WHO IS IN THE CAR
- A "Sharing this car" card shows everyone in the ride: their first name,
  whether they are a man or a woman, and where they get in and out
- It appears the moment somebody joins, so you hear it from us rather than
  finding a stranger in the back seat
- The invite screen shows the same before you decide, along with your driver's
  name, car and plate
- Something not right? The driver is one tap away

💵 YOUR FARE, NOT THE WHOLE CAR'S
- Your ride screen now shows what YOU pay, not the fare for the whole car
- A shared-ride banner tells you how many are aboard, how many seats are free,
  and that your share falls as more people join

🚗 FOR DRIVERS — YOU FINALLY KNOW YOUR CAR
- A passenger list before pickup: who you are collecting, how many, and what
  each of them owes
- Every passenger who joins after you accept is pushed to you by name
- During the ride you drop people off ONE AT A TIME, each by name, with their
  own fare and a running total of what is still to collect
- Can't find someone at the kerb? Call them from the list
- Before this, the first passenger you dropped ended everybody's ride

📊 WHAT ELSE IMPROVED
- Signing up on a shared phone no longer makes people wait behind each other —
  a new number's first code always goes through
- The update notice now tells you what actually changed instead of offering you
  the version you are already running

🚀 NEXT UP
- Driver voice booking
- App-wide security attestation (App Check)
- More payment options

Available now in your Velocity app! 🎉

Questions? Tap Support from Settings for help.
