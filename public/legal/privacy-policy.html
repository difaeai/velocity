Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Velocity Rides mobile app collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have over your data.

Effective: 25 October 2026 Applies to: Velocity Rides for Android & iOS, and Velocity Studio Contact: business@velocityrides.app

1 Who we are

Velocity Rides is a product of Berreto (Private) Limited (berreto.com). Berreto (Private) Limited is the company responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Berreto (Private) Limited. We operate Velocity Rides (“Velocity”), a ride-hailing and shared-travel platform that connects riders with drivers. This Privacy Policy covers the Velocity Rides mobile application and related services. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2 Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service safely. This includes:

Information you provide

  • Phone number — used to create your account and sign you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Profile details — your name and, where relevant, your role (rider or driver).
  • Driver verification documents — if you register as a driver, photos of identity and vehicle documents you upload for verification, captured with your camera or chosen from your photo library.
  • Partner verification documents — if you apply to the Earn with Velocity partner program, photos of your CNIC and, for paid tiers, a screenshot of your registration-fee payment.
  • Payment screenshots — when you settle a commission or outstanding fee, the payment-receipt screenshot you upload as proof of transfer. These images may show the amount, the sending account name or number, and the receiving account.
  • Chat and community content — messages, photos, shared locations, and contact details you choose to send in trip chat, group chats, or direct messages, and posts you publish to the community feed. If you use the in-chat contact-sharing feature, the app accesses your contacts only to let you pick one; your contact list is never uploaded or stored.

Information collected automatically

  • Location — precise and approximate location is used while the app is in use to set pickup points, match nearby rides, show drivers on the map, and enable live trip tracking. Location is collected only when the app is open and you are using a location feature.
  • Nearby activity on the map — while the home screen is open, your approximate area is used to show other Velocity users that there is activity around them, as an anonymous dot. This is not live tracking and it does not identify you: the position is deliberately shifted by a few hundred metres before anyone sees it, and it carries no name, phone number, photo, account identifier, trip or fare. Nobody can tell which dot is you, message a dot, or look up who it belongs to. Your last known area stops being shown once you stop opening the app.
  • Saved routes — a route you save under "Location & time" is counted in "Locations people are traveling to", which riders and drivers near you can see. They see only its pickup and drop-off areas (for example "F-7" to "Blue Area"), the average time people on it leave, and how many men and women travel it — never your address, exact location, name or any account identifier. Delete the route and it stops being counted.
  • Device & diagnostic information — a device push token and basic technical data needed to deliver notifications and keep the app working reliably.
  • Trip activity — pickup and drop-off points, trip status, and fare estimates generated while you use the service.
  • Ride dash cam video — while a ride is in progress, the driver's phone records video and sound of the inside of the car with its front camera. Riders are told on the trip screen before they get in. See section 8.
  • Voice input — if you use voice booking, audio is captured only while the microphone is running and is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service. Velocity does not store the audio. See section 6.
  • Advertising ID — for users who are not on a paid plan, your device's Google advertising identifier is used to serve and measure ads. See section 7.

Payments

Rides may be paid in cash or, where enabled, through supported local payment methods. Velocity does not collect or store your full card or bank credentials; regulated payment details are handled by the applicable payment provider.

3 How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account and verify drivers.
  • Match riders and drivers and calculate estimated fares.
  • Show live location and trip progress during a ride.
  • Show, anonymously, whether there are cars and other users around you.
  • Send trip updates, account alerts, and service notifications.
  • Verify commission and fee payments, including with the automated (AI) check described in section 5.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and keep the community safe.
  • Diagnose problems and improve app reliability.

We do not sell your personal information. We do show third-party advertising from Google AdMob to users who are not on a paid plan, which uses your device's advertising ID — section 7 sets out exactly what is shared, how to turn off ad personalisation, and how to remove ads altogether.

4 How information is shared

  • Between riders and drivers — when a trip is arranged, we share only the limited details needed to complete it (such as first name, pickup area, and live location during the ride).
  • Dash cam video — a ride's recording can be watched by its driver and by the people who rode in the car, each for their own part of the ride, for 24 hours after it ends. See section 8.
  • Service providers — we use trusted providers to run the app, including Google Firebase (notifications and app infrastructure), Google Maps (mapping and location), and Anthropic (automated payment-screenshot verification, section 5). They process data only to provide these services to us and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes, including training AI models.
  • Advertising — for users who are not on a paid plan, Google AdMob receives your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Unlike the providers above, Google uses that data under its own privacy policy rather than solely on our instructions. See section 7.
  • Speech recognition — if you use voice booking, the audio goes to your device's speech-recognition service (Google's, on most Android phones) to be turned into text. See section 6.
  • Legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users and the public.

5 Automated payment verification (AI)

When you upload a payment screenshot to settle a driver commission or an outstanding cancellation fee, that screenshot is analysed by an AI vision model operated by Anthropic to check that it shows a genuine receipt, for the right amount, sent to a Velocity account. This is disclosed on the upload screen at the moment you submit.

  • What is shared — only the screenshot you upload and the amount and account it should match. No other account data, location, or chat content is sent.
  • Limited use — Anthropic processes the image solely to return a verification result to us. Under our API terms it is not used to train AI models.
  • Human review — only a clear, confident match is approved automatically. Anything uncertain is decided by a member of our team, and if your payment is declined you can contest the decision by contacting business@velocityrides.app — a person, not the AI, makes the final call.

If you prefer not to have your screenshot processed this way, contact support and we will verify your payment manually.

6 Voice booking & the microphone

You can book a ride by speaking instead of typing, and drivers can run a small set of spoken commands. This is optional — everything it does is also available on screen.

  • The microphone runs only when you start it. Audio is captured from the moment you tap the microphone button until recognition finishes. There is no background listening and no wake word.
  • Speech becomes text using your device's recogniser. The audio is handed to the speech-recognition service built into your phone. On most Android devices that service is Google's, and it may send the audio to Google's servers under Google's own privacy policy. Velocity does not record, upload, or keep the audio, and never sends it to a Velocity server.
  • Only the recognised text is used. The words are interpreted on your device to fill in a destination, pickup point, seat count, or vehicle type. We keep no transcript beyond the booking you go on to confirm.
  • Turning it off. Decline the microphone permission, or revoke it later in Android settings. The rest of the app is unaffected.

7 Advertising

Velocity shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. They are what keeps the app free to use.

  • Who sees ads. Only users without a paid entitlement. An active Travel Partner subscription, or a current Pro partner tier, removes ads across the whole app — not just the section it was bought in.
  • What Google receives. Your device's advertising ID, together with the technical data needed to serve and measure an ad. Google uses this under its own privacy policy rather than solely on our instructions. We do not send Google your phone number, your name, your trips, or your chat content, and we do not combine the advertising ID with your Velocity account data.
  • Consent. Where a choice is legally required — including the EEA and the UK — a consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and no ads load if consent is withheld. Ads are limited to a PG content rating.
  • Your controls. Android lets you delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so stops personalised ads; you may still see non-personalised ones.

8 Ride dash cam

Every ride is recorded by a dash cam running on the driver's phone, so that riders and drivers both have proof if something goes wrong during the ride.

  • What is recorded. Video from the phone's front camera, which faces the inside of the car, and sound if the driver has allowed the microphone. Recording runs only while a ride is in progress and the Velocity Rides app is open on the driver's phone.
  • Who can watch it. The driver, and the people who rode in the car. In a shared ride each rider can watch only the part of the ride they were in the car for. Nobody else can open it from the app.
  • How long it is kept. 24 hours after the ride ends. The video is then deleted automatically. Anyone entitled to watch it can save a copy of a clip to their own phone during those 24 hours.
  • The camera check. When a ride starts, a few frames of the recording are checked by our own software, on our own servers, to count whether any face can be seen, so the app can tell the driver when the phone is not placed where it can see the cabin. Only the number of faces is used. Faces are not identified or matched to anyone, and nothing from this check is kept.
  • Turning it off. The dash cam is part of how rides work: a driver cannot start a ride without allowing the camera. Sound is optional.

14 Velocity Studio (for businesses)

Velocity Studio is a separate service for businesses, at velocityrides.app/studio. If you sign up for it:

  • What you give us. Your email address, your business name and a short description of your business, its customers, market and language. If you pay, the transaction ID or receipt screenshot you send.
  • Facebook & Instagram. When you connect your accounts with Facebook Login, we receive an access token for the Facebook Page and Instagram professional account you choose, and — only if you connect the ads desk — your Meta ad account. We use them only to do what you ask: publish the posts and reels the service makes, read and answer comments on them, read your posts’ performance and your profile to improve future posts, and create, read and manage the ad campaigns you approve. Tokens are stored encrypted on our servers and are never shown to anyone, including you.
  • Sales agent. If you use it, we store the businesses it finds (from public maps and websites) and the emails it writes. If you connect a mailbox, its app password is stored encrypted and used only to send the emails you approved and the follow-ups you allowed, and to read replies from the businesses it wrote to. The inbox is opened read-only: only messages from those businesses are read, nothing is marked read, moved or deleted, and we keep the text of their reply so an answer can be drafted for you.
  • Market analysis. Anything you type about your business, and the public pages you give us links to, are read to analyse your market and are kept in your workspace.
  • AI processing. Your business description, what you tell us, your posts’ text, the replies your leads send and public market information are sent to Google Gemini to write and plan content. We do not send rider or driver data, and we do not sell your data.
  • Your control. Disconnect an account at any time from Connected accounts, which deletes its stored token. To delete your Studio workspace and everything in it, email business@velocityrides.app from the address you signed up with; we delete it within 30 days. You can also remove our app from Facebook at Settings → Business Integrations.
