Delete your account & data

This page explains how to request deletion of your Velocity Rides account and the personal data associated with it.

App: Velocity Rides Developer: Velocity Rides Effective: 8 July 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at support@velocityrides.app with the subject line “Account Deletion Request”.
  2. Include your registered mobile number (the phone number you use to sign in) so we can verify the request belongs to you.
  3. We verify and delete. Once verified, we will delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days and confirm by email.
Send your request to
support@velocityrides.app
