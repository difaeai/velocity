3 What we sell

Two different things, billed differently.

Rides Car, bike, courier and shared intercity rides in Pakistan, booked in the Velocity Rides app. You pay the fare for the ride you took, in rupees, in cash or from your Velocity balance. There is no subscription and no booking fee for a rider.
Velocity Studio — Vexa A social media team for a business, sold by the month or by the year, in US dollars: $20 (Research Assistant), $26 (Content) or $32 (Social Media Manager + Meta & Google Ad Expert) a month. A year is the same price less 3%. Each payment buys exactly that one month or one year — nothing renews on its own and no card is kept on file to be charged again.

4 How you can pay

For Velocity Studio: a transfer to our bank account, or a debit or credit card.

Card payments are taken by Stripe on Stripe's own page. We never see, handle or store your card number, and your card is not saved for later unless you tell us otherwise. Your statement will show the amount in US dollars; your own bank decides the exchange rate it uses.

Rides are paid in rupees, in cash to the driver or from your in-app balance.

5 Refunds and cancellations

Velocity Studio. Nothing renews automatically, so stopping is simply not paying again. If you ask for your money back before the team has started work, we refund the payment in full, to the same card or account it came from, within 7 working days of agreeing it. Once the team has started work for that month or year, that period is not refunded, but nothing further is charged and your access runs to the end of what you paid for.

Rides. Cancelling before a driver has set off costs nothing. A cancellation fee is only charged where the app told you so at the time, and it is shown to you before it applies. If you were charged for a ride you did not take, or the fare looks wrong, tell us within 7 days through Support in the app or by email and we will check it against the trip record and put it right.

Chargebacks. If something looks wrong on your statement, write to us first — we can usually settle it the same day, which is faster than a bank dispute.
