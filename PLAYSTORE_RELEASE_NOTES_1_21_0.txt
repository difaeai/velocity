APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on a NEW 1.21.0 version record)
-----------------------------------------------------------
TRAVEL PARTNER, SIMPLER

Travel Partner now opens straight to people going your way. It uses the profile
you made when you signed up, so there is nothing new to fill in.

CHOOSE WHO CAN REACH YOU

A new Open / Mute / Close switch sits at the top of Travel Partner. Open: anyone
can like or message you and you are notified. Mute: they still can, quietly.
Close: nobody can like or message you until you open again.

GROUPS GET THEIR OWN TAB

Commute groups have their own tab. Your matches, chats and ride links are
together in your profile.

FASTER RIDES

Drivers accept a ride at your fare at once, picked-up calls no longer hang up,
and each rider on a shared ride starts and ends their own trip.

ALSO

The home map shows where people are travelling. Cancellation fees and no-shows
are recorded in Ride History. Drivers' face checks run on the phone and are
quicker.
