APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.18.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

SOS NOW REACHES THE PEOPLE WHO CAN ACTUALLY COME

Pressing SOS used to tell Velocity and nobody else. It now opens one sheet that
dials police 15, alerts our desk, and hands you a WhatsApp message to each of
your own five emergency contacts with your location, your driver and the plate
already written in. The messages go from YOUR number, not ours — an 11pm
message from an unknown business number is the one people ignore. There is a
one-tap SMS fallback that works with no internet.

WHO IS WAITING ON YOUR ROAD

Book a shared ride that nobody joins and the screen used to show you a
countdown and a fare that was never going to move. It now shows the riders
standing on the road you are about to drive down — the areas they get in and
out, never doorsteps — and what your own fare becomes if the driver picks them
up. It shows nothing at all when the road is quiet, which is most of the time.

KNOW WHAT IS ON YOUR ROAD BEFORE YOU BOOK

Velocity reads fourteen Pakistani news feeds and tells you what is closed,
blocked or crashed between you and where you are going. For Islamabad and
Rawalpindi that includes the traffic police's own diversion advisories, which
arrive within the hour they are issued.

It only speaks when it has something to say, and only while the problem is
actually happening. A protest announced in the morning for two in the
afternoon appears shortly before two and is gone by evening — not sitting on
your screen all day over a road that is clear. When a newspaper gives the
hours, you see them: "till 6pm". When there is nothing wrong, the strip says
so quietly, so its silence means something.

It names the newspaper and links the story every time. It never tells you how
many minutes you will lose, because we read newspapers, not traffic sensors.

THE PUMP PRICE BEHIND YOUR FARE

The fuel price your fare is built on is now read every morning from PSO's own
price page, alongside two independent sources. Nothing is believed until two
of them agree, so a single site having a bad day cannot move what you pay.

YOUR RIDES, AND THE PEOPLE IN THEM

You can see your own ride record, and on a shared ride you can rate the people
you travelled with — privately. Drivers get the same record for their own
trips.

FOR DRIVERS

You can verify yourself inside the app: upload your papers, then a live face
scan. Real documents are no longer refused — a National Highways & Motorway
Police permit and both Islamabad Traffic Police licence layouts used to be
read as a CNIC and auto-rejected. Your car is shown back to you once it is
approved. And you can top up by bank transfer, with the receipt read
automatically.

A MENU YOU CAN READ AT A GLANCE

Every item in the Velocity menu has been redrawn as a small colour
illustration of the thing it stands for, and they animate into place as the
menu opens. City rides, Special Rides and sharing rides no longer share one
face.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE BUILD vc44 / iOS 17 (for the team; not store copy)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
10 commits, 17 Oct 2026 — PRs #46-#50, all merged to main, CI green on 86747a7.

RIDER- AND DRIVER-VISIBLE
  - SOS reaches the rider's own emergency contacts. Police 15, our desk, and
    five WhatsApp deep links sent from the rider's OWN number, plus an SMS
    fallback that needs no internet. WhatsApp cannot send on somebody's behalf,
    so the cost is honest: five deep links, five presses. An unconfirmed
    contact is still alerted — refusing to warn a family over a missing
    checkbox would be absurd — but the screen keeps asking.
  - Riders see who else is on their corridor, and what it does to their fare.
    The same corridor search the driver's en-route feed uses, from the rider's
    seat; every candidate has already passed all four corridor gates, seats and
    the gender rules server-side. Note that an en-route pickup usually does NOT
    cut the host's fare — the tier ceiling means the gain goes to the driver —
    so the card only shows what the server actually computes.
  - The driver document reader stopped rejecting real Pakistani papers. A NHMP
    "NATIONAL DRIVING PERMIT" was scoring as a CNIC front (it opens "ISLAMIC
    REPUBLIC OF PAKISTAN" and says "Date of Birth"), and on an ordinary phone
    photo of a laminated card the tie went to CNIC — `document_wrong_type` is
    FATAL in decideDriverVerification, so a real driver with real papers was
    auto-rejected. Decisive markers settle it now, with the real card formats
    in tests.

PARTNER PROGRAM
  - Both plans are paid: Starter Rs 3,000/month against Pro's Rs 4,500, sold in
    the same 3, 6 and 12-month blocks, and every application now carries a plan
    length and a payment screenshot. The tier id stays `free` on purpose — it is
    stamped on every partner doc, settled partner transaction and fleet record,
    and renaming it buys a nicer word in the database in exchange for a
    migration. The UI says Starter, the data says `free`.
  - A lapsed plan earns nothing (loadEdge drops the edge), but A MISSING EXPIRY
    NEVER LAPSES: the partners who joined while the lower tier was genuinely
    free carry no proExpiresAt and keep earning until an admin stamps one on
    them. Switching off somebody's income as a side effect of a deploy is not a
    thing to do silently.
  - One monthlyFeeForTier() serves the apply screen, the validator and the admin
    desk, so they cannot disagree about the price — which is the bug proFee vs
    proMonthlyFee already caused once.

BACKEND AND ADMIN ONLY — nothing in the store copy
  - Velocity tests itself: a feature-contract registry (qa/features.ts), a
    15-minute agent, a watcher in the api client, and an admin Testing page.
    Passes write nothing; the logs are prompt-shaped.
  - Road alerts are proved SERVER-SIDE, not by the app watcher, because
    getTrafficAlerts answers {ok:true} with empty lists when it cannot read
    anything at all — a fast, successful, empty call looks identical whether
    the feature is healthy or completely dead. Two agent checks ask whether the
    20-minute refresh is still running and whether the alerts being held reach
    anybody. `platform.traffic` is deliberately NOT in CALLABLE_FEATURE and
    three files now say why; getSuggestedRides carries the same warning for the
    same reason.
  - An admin page for the accounts money arrives in. adminSetSettlementAccounts
    had been live for months with no caller anywhere, so config/settlementAccounts
    sat empty in production — which silently broke three things at once: a
    commission-locked driver was told the payment account is not set up and
    could never clear the lock, the top-up sheet had no account to show, and
    parseRecipientMatch had nothing to compare a receipt against, so every
    receipt failed the recipient check. Saving goes through a confirm step,
    because a typo here sends somebody's commission to a stranger's wallet.
    PAYMENTS.md now warns that the document is load-bearing.
