APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.18.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

KNOW WHAT IS ON YOUR ROAD BEFORE YOU BOOK

Velocity now reads fourteen Pakistani news feeds and tells you what is closed,
blocked or crashed between you and where you are going. For Islamabad and
Rawalpindi that includes the traffic police's own diversion advisories, which
arrive within the hour they are issued.

It only speaks when it has something to say, and only while the problem is
actually happening. A protest announced in the morning for two in the
afternoon appears shortly before two and is gone by evening — not sitting on
your screen all day over a road that is clear. When a newspaper gives the
hours, you see them: "till 6pm". When there is nothing wrong, the strip says
so quietly, so its silence means something.

It names the newspaper and links the story every time. It never tells you how
many minutes you will lose, because we read newspapers, not traffic sensors.

THE PUMP PRICE BEHIND YOUR FARE

The fuel price your fare is built on is now read every morning from PSO's own
price page, alongside two independent sources. Nothing is believed until two
of them agree, so a single site having a bad day cannot move what you pay.

YOUR RIDES, AND THE PEOPLE IN THEM

You can see your own ride record, and on a shared ride you can rate the people
you travelled with — privately. Drivers get the same record for their own
trips.

FOR DRIVERS

You can now verify yourself inside the app: upload your papers, then a live
face scan. And you can top up by bank transfer, with the receipt read
automatically.

A MENU YOU CAN READ AT A GLANCE

Every item in the Velocity menu has been redrawn as a small colour
illustration of the thing it stands for, and they animate into place as the
menu opens. City rides, Special Rides and sharing rides no longer share one
face.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD (for the team; not store copy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
27 commits, 13–17 Oct 2026. Grouped by what a release manager needs to know.

RIDER- AND DRIVER-VISIBLE
  - Road alerts (`backend/functions/src/traffic/`, `src/ui/TrafficStrip.tsx`).
    Fourteen news feeds, ~99% of items refused. Wired into the rider's booking
    screen and both driver screens, reusing the memoised route so no extra
    Routes call is bought.
  - Road alerts are WINDOW-GATED (PR #44). `window.ts` reads the hours out of
    the event sentence and the alert shows only inside them, with 90 minutes of
    lead. Two new refusals: an event already over, and one more than a week
    out. A story that states no hours behaves exactly as before.
  - Islamabad/Rawalpindi got their own sources. APP (the state news agency)
    carries ITP advisories verbatim; The Nation's Islamabad feed and Daily
    Times join it. ChampAlertsOnTheGo — which is what was actually asked for —
    publishes on X and Instagram only and has no readable site at all; the
    probe is recorded in `sources.ts`.
  - Fuel prices read from psopk.com as a third, first-party source, and the
    daily job moved 06:15 → 06:00. PSO does NOT get a casting vote: on the day
    it was added it was a full day behind OGRA (390.23 vs the mirrors' 386.30),
    which is in a regression test now.
  - Ride record + rider-to-rider ratings. Co-rider ratings are not users.rating
    and are anonymous except to an admin.
  - Driver verification engine with a live face scan — our own detection, no
    API key, no third party. Papers first, then the face. The daily face check
    ships OFF behind a flag.
  - Bank-transfer top-ups, read by our own OCR engine. Entry is the Earnings
    commission card, not the hidden wallet.

BACKEND AND ADMIN ONLY — nothing in the store copy
  - Social desk: a second engine that writes without a paid key, Instagram via
    Facebook Login, self-learning from its own page, reels, briefs. Locally
    written pieces never auto-publish.
  - Admin page for the accounts drivers pay into.
  - The marketing site ships its own fonts, so a build never waits on Google.
  - Fixes worth remembering: `paymentProofAI` referenced `createHash` with no
    import and broke main; a missing WASM binary now degrades instead of
    crashing; driver verification runs on a schedule, not a Firestore trigger
    (see the Eventarc IAM block); one renamed social metric was zeroing every
    post's numbers.
