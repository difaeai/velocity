APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.18.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

A MENU YOU CAN READ AT A GLANCE

Every item in the Velocity menu has been redrawn. Instead of thin grey
outlines that all looked alike, each one is now a small colour illustration of
the thing it stands for — a parcel for Couriers, a folded map for your saved
routes, a lifebuoy for help, a card coming out of a wallet.

They also move. As the menu slides open, the wheels roll, the pin drops on the
map, the letter comes out of the envelope and the settings cog turns itself
into place. Press and hold any row and its icon answers you.

Three products used to share one face. City rides, Special Rides and sharing
rides are now unmistakably three different things, which matters most when you
are opening the menu to find one of them in a hurry.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD (for the team; not store copy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
One branch off main, 13 Oct 2026.

  - src/ui/DrawerIcons.tsx rewritten. 17 icons, drawn on a 48-unit grid (was
    24) as shaded objects in real materials — kraft, gold, steel, paper,
    glass — rather than 1.6px two-tone outlines. The brand lime is kept for
    the single detail that names each feature (headlights, destination pin,
    parcel tape, magnifier, tick), never the whole object.

  - The five marks the drawer used to borrow from ServiceIcons/RideIcons
    (city car, city-to-city, parcel, shared ride, wallet) are now drawn in
    DrawerIcons too, so the menu is one family. Home still uses the originals;
    the shapes are the same objects, so nothing changes identity.

  - Special Rides is no longer a car. City rides is a car and sharing rides is
    a car, and a third car with a badge on it recreates the exact problem the
    emoji had. It is an award rosette now. (A chauffeur's cap was drawn first
    and rendered as a flying saucer at 26px — a dome on a disc is a dome on a
    disc.)

  - Motion: each row passes its icon ONE value, Animated.add(the row's slice
    of the existing entrance cascade, the row's press value) — 0 arriving, 1
    at rest, 2 held. No new timers, no new Animated.Values, nothing loops. The
    moving parts are native-driven View layers over the still art, because a
    transform inside an <Svg> cannot run on the native driver and sixteen JS
    animations would fight the scroll.

  - The icon tile grew 38 → 42px and the art 21 → 26px. Shading needs pixels
    to read as shading; that size increase is half of the legibility fix.

  - PassengerDrawer's <Cascade> now takes a progress value instead of
    (stagger, index); rowProgress() is the one place the slice is computed, so
    the row and its icon cannot drift apart.

  Not touched: the driver's menu still runs on emoji. Same argument applies to
  it, but "rest is fine" was the instruction.

  Verified by rasterising the real source out of DrawerIcons.tsx to a contact
  sheet at drawer size (sharp), not by eye on the paths. Three icons failed
  that check and were redrawn: the cap (saucer), the avatar stack (the
  shoulder dome swallowed the head, so every face was a cloud) and sign-out (a
  rounded slab reads as a phone, not a door).

  tsc clean. Mobile lint reports only the pre-existing react-hooks/refs
  pattern, all 23 in PassengerDrawer, none in the new file.
