VELOCITY RIDES 1.11.0  ·  Android versionCode 36  ·  iOS build 9
=================================================================

1.10.0 (vc35) was built on 2026-09-24 and never rolled out, so everything it
carried ships here as well. On iOS this follows build 8, which was also never
submitted; the last release actually live on the App Store is 1.9.0 (build 7)
from 2026-09-22.

Almost all of this release is invisible by design. It is a performance, battery
and cost pass over the app plus the groundwork for Velocity's own map — not a
feature drop. The honest summary is "the same app, lighter".


PLAY CONSOLE "WHAT'S NEW" (copy the block below, max 500 chars)
---------------------------------------------------------------
This one is about speed and battery.

The app no longer works in the background when you are not looking at it, so it
stops draining your battery and your data. Long chats open instantly instead of
loading every message you have ever sent. Maps and route lines draw straight
away when you come back to a screen.

Velocity has also started learning the places Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi
actually use, so finding your destination will keep getting quicker.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.10.0
-------------------------

Battery and mobile data — the app stops when you do
  Four screens were quietly polling our servers every 15 to 30 seconds and did
  not stop when you switched away from the app. Backgrounding an app does not
  stop its timers, so a driver with the phone in their pocket kept making a
  round trip every few seconds with nobody looking at the answer.

  Those four — the en-route rider search, suggested rides, the sharing feed and
  the pool manifest — now pause the moment the app leaves the screen and refresh
  the instant you come back. Coming back is actually fresher than before, because
  the old always-on version had no idea you had returned.

  Deliberately NOT paused: the driver's location upload. That feeds your "online"
  status and the moving car the passenger watches, so it has to keep running while
  a driver is mid-trip with Google Maps in front of the app. Pausing it would have
  broken live tracking, which is exactly the sort of thing a battery optimisation
  is not allowed to cost.

Long chats open instantly
  Every conversation used to load its entire history on open — every message it
  had ever held, re-rendered whenever anybody typed. A Travel Partner group a few
  months old therefore got slower every week. Chats now load the most recent 200
  messages. Under 200, which is nearly all of them, nothing changes at all.

  Same for post comments, the support chat, the driver trip chat and your
  notifications list.

Maps and route lines come back instantly
  One ride used to ask Google for the same road up to four separate times — the
  booking screen, the trip screen, the driver's en-route screen and the request
  detail screen each fetched it again, because the cache died with the screen.
  Now the road is fetched once and every screen after that draws it on the first
  frame instead of showing a straight line and correcting itself.

Address search
  Fewer, better-timed lookups: search now starts at three characters instead of
  two (two characters match half the country and nobody picks a result from
  them), waits a moment longer for you to stop typing, and remembers what you
  searched a second ago so backspacing and retyping is instant.

  Underneath, Velocity has started building its own map of the places people
  actually use. Every completed ride teaches it where a destination really is —
  from the pickup and the drop-off — and once enough separate rides agree on a
  place, the app can find it without asking anyone else. It holds nothing on
  launch day and fills as rides happen, so expect this to get better over weeks
  rather than on install.

One less permission
  The app asked Android for permission to WRITE to your contacts. It never
  wrote a contact and never will — sharing a contact in chat only reads the one
  you pick, through Android's own picker. The permission is gone.

Driver demand map
  The demand map was streaming every open request in the country to every driver
  who opened it. It now reads a bounded sample, which is the same picture at 20x14
  tiles and a fraction of the work.

Known trade-off
  The two ETA badges on a driver's request-detail screen are now free-flow
  estimates rather than live-traffic ones. Fares are worked out from distance and
  are unaffected, and the corridor match is geometry, so nothing about money or
  matching changes — but in heavy traffic those two numbers will read optimistic.
  Traffic-aware routing costs double and half the free allowance, and it was
  being paid for on every route line in the app for two numbers on one screen.
  It is now a one-line switch if that trade turns out wrong.


BACKEND AND ADMIN (not in the app, shipped alongside)
-----------------------------------------------------

  - Address lookups moved off Places Text Search onto the Geocoding API: the same
    answer for $5 per 1,000 calls instead of $32. Route lookups moved off the
    traffic-aware tier for the same reason.
  - Answers are cached the way Google's licence actually permits: coordinates for
    under 30 days with an enforced expiry and a daily sweep, place IDs kept
    indefinitely because those are expressly exempt. Keeping the two apart is what
    makes a repeat lookup cheap instead of full price.
  - New admin desk at Locations (/dashboard/locations) for Velocity's own map:
    verify, rename, alias or merge the places the platform learns from real rides.
  - Fixed: the nightly Travel Partner subscription expiry had been failing on a
    missing Firestore index, which meant expired subscriptions stayed active and
    kept their paid swipe allowance.
  - Fixed: a scheduled job was running at 08:00 Karachi rather than 03:00 because
    it had no timezone set.


AFTER THE ROLLOUT — DO NOT SKIP
-------------------------------
  1. Roll out in Play Console. Building an AAB is not releasing it; vc31, vc34
     and vc35 were all built and never rolled out.
  2. Then publish the version in Admin → App version, INCLUDING the build number
     (36 for Android, 9 for iOS under the `ios` fields — never the Play fields).
     An install only prompts when what is published is strictly newer than what it
     is running, and since our releases often keep a version string, the build
     number is the field that does the work. Publishing early asks people to fetch
     an update the store cannot serve them yet.
  3. iOS is submitted separately with the patch-submit-restore script.
