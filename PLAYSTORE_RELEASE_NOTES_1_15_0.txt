APP STORE "WHAT'S NEW" (set on version 1.15.0 through the API)
--------------------------------------------------------------
NEW IN THIS VERSION

FOR DRIVERS: COMMISSION IS HALVED, AND A FULL DAY IS FREE

Velocity Rides' commission is now 5% of the cash you collect, down from 10%,
and it is charged by the day.

Complete 16 pool rides in one day and that whole day costs you no commission at
all. Fall short and the day costs 5% of the cash you took that day, payable
after midnight — you clear it by transfer with a screenshot, and your account
unlocks as soon as it is verified.

Nothing is ever owed while the day is still running. You have the full day to
reach the target, and your app shows the live count, every condition still
missing, and exactly what the day will cost you if you stop now. Only pool and
sharing rides count toward the target; a solo ride still earns you its fare.

The separate PKR 2,000 daily bonus is gone, replaced by the free day it was
paying for.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD (for the team; not store copy)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Merged 760a537 off main, 6 Oct 2026.

  - Commission rate 10% → 5%, and the unit of charging is now a PAKISTAN DAY
    rather than a running cycle. 16 qualifying POOL rides in a day → that day
    owes nothing; fewer → the day owes 5% of its cash, and that becomes payable
    the moment the day closes at midnight. The driver cannot accept new work
    until they clear it.
  - The PKR 5,000 settle threshold is GONE and must not come back. At intercity
    fares it fired at about the fourth ride of the day, so it would have locked
    every driver short of the sixteen it was meant to reward. Nothing is owed
    mid-day now.
  - The separate PKR 2,000 bonus is removed. `dailyTargetBonus` survives as an
    admin lever at 0 and every bonus surface in the app hides itself while it
    is zero.
  - Only pool / sharing rides count (`dailyTargetPoolOnly`, admin-togglable,
    on). A solo ride still earns its fare and still owes its commission; it
    just does not move the counter.
  - Which day a fare belongs to is DERIVED, not swept: three new driver fields
    (`cycleDay`, `cycleGrossToday`, `cycleCashToday`) make the open day and the
    closed days separable at read time. At 23:59 a short day locks nobody; at
    00:00 the same figures lock the driver, with no write in between and no job
    in the money path. A driver mid-cycle at deploy has no `cycleDay`, so their
    cycle reads as open — one day of grace.
  - `notifyClosedCommissionDays` runs 00:05 Asia/Karachi and pushes the drivers
    whose day closed owing, with the amount. It writes no money fields; the
    lock does not depend on it.
  - `granted` on the day document replaces `bonusGranted > 0` as the
    once-per-day guard. With the bonus at 0 that test is always false, and the
    waiver would have been re-granted on every ride after the sixteenth.
  - The day-roll and the auto-clear now live in one place
    (`drivers/cycle.ts`). completeTrip and completePoolRide had written that
    arithmetic separately and only one of them cleared a cycle the bonus had
    covered.
  - Settling removes the closed days and KEEPS the open one, which may yet cost
    the driver nothing. Account deletion is the one place the open day is also
    charged, or "delete account" is the cheapest way to skip a day.
  - Driver app, admin Commission page, support AI brief, public site FAQ and
    the Urdu strings all follow. Admin page refuses a target that rewards
    nothing (waiver off AND bonus 0).

Verified before the build: backend 776 tests / 55 files, mobile 150 tests
(12 of them new, pinning the app's copy of the rule against the backend's),
backend + mobile + web typechecks clean, eslint clean. CI green on 760a537 and
the Cloud Functions deploy succeeded.

`config/commissionSettings` does not exist in production, so the shipped
defaults are what apply — no migration and nothing to re-save in the admin.
