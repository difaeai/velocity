VELOCITY 1.8.0  ·  versionCode 30
=================================

PLAY CONSOLE "WHAT'S NEW" (copy the block below, max 500 chars)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Shared rides now work end to end: start a pool and other riders can find and
join it while it is still looking for a driver, and once a driver takes the
ride they approve anyone new before the car changes.

Also: a bigger, easier "Where to?" on the home screen, you can delete your
account from inside the app, and Travel Partner notifications now actually
arrive.


WHAT CHANGED SINCE 1.7.2
------------------------

Shared rides finally have a whole journey
  A pool used to be invisible until a driver took it, so the rider who started
  one rode alone while everybody on the same road never learned it existed.
  Then, once a driver did take it, anyone could be added to their car without
  being asked.

  Both ends are fixed, and who decides what is now stated once and enforced
  everywhere:

    - The rider who STARTS a pool owns the fare. They name the offer and take
      or refuse a driver's bid. Everyone who joins later takes the per-seat
      price as it stands — there is no second negotiation inside a pool.
    - The DRIVER owns the car. Before one is confirmed there is nobody to ask,
      so joining is instant. After a driver has agreed to carry the ride, a new
      passenger is a change to THEIR work, so they accept or reject it from
      their trip card.

  A pool now also stays discoverable for ten minutes after booking while it
  hunts for a driver, and you no longer have to type a destination before you
  can see whether a cheap seat exists nearby.

Home screen
  "Where to?" is bigger — both the heading and the box — and voice booking has
  become a mic tile beside it instead of a full row of its own.

Delete your account from inside the app
  Settings now carries account deletion, rather than sending people to a web
  page to ask for it.

Travel Partner notifications were going nowhere
  Travel Partner had been writing notifications that were never delivered.
  They arrive now.

Sign-in codes can come over WhatsApp
  Verification codes are sent as a WhatsApp message where that is possible, and
  as an SMS everywhere it is not. Nothing about signing in changes for anyone —
  same six digits, same screen — and the app says which one it used so nobody
  is left watching the wrong inbox.

  This is a cost change rather than a feature: an SMS to a Pakistani number
  costs several times what the same code costs on WhatsApp. SMS is not going
  away and is not behind a switch; it is what carries every login WhatsApp
  cannot.

iOS
  Groundwork only, invisible on Android: the app now builds and passes review
  on iOS.
