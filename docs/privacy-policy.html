Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Velocity Rides mobile app collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have over your data.

Effective: 8 July 2026 Applies to: Velocity Rides for Android & iOS Contact: berreto01@gmail.com

1 Who we are

Velocity Rides (“Velocity”, “we”, “us”) operates a ride-hailing and shared-travel platform that connects riders with drivers. This Privacy Policy covers the Velocity Rides mobile application and related services. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2 Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service safely. This includes:

Information you provide

  • Phone number — used to create your account and sign you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Profile details — your name and, where relevant, your role (rider or driver).
  • Driver verification documents — if you register as a driver, photos of identity and vehicle documents you upload for verification, captured with your camera or chosen from your photo library.

Information collected automatically

  • Location — precise and approximate location is used while the app is in use to set pickup points, match nearby rides, show drivers on the map, and enable live trip tracking. Location is collected only when the app is open and you are using a location feature.
  • Device & diagnostic information — a device push token and basic technical data needed to deliver notifications and keep the app working reliably.
  • Trip activity — pickup and drop-off points, trip status, and fare estimates generated while you use the service.

Payments

Rides may be paid in cash or, where enabled, through supported local payment methods. Velocity does not collect or store your full card or bank credentials; regulated payment details are handled by the applicable payment provider.

3 How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account and verify drivers.
  • Match riders and drivers and calculate estimated fares.
  • Show live location and trip progress during a ride.
  • Send trip updates, account alerts, and service notifications.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and keep the community safe.
  • Diagnose problems and improve app reliability.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4 How information is shared

  • Between riders and drivers — when a trip is arranged, we share only the limited details needed to complete it (such as first name, pickup area, and live location during the ride).
  • Service providers — we use trusted providers to run the app, including Google Firebase (notifications and app infrastructure) and Google Maps (mapping and location). They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • Legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users and the public.

5 Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

6 Your rights & account deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

To delete your account and associated data, email berreto01@gmail.com from your registered email or with your registered phone number. We will verify the request and delete your account and personal data, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

7 Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

8 Children

Velocity Rides is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

9 Third-party services

Our app relies on third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Maps — policies.google.com/privacy

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Effective” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
